# Among Us 3D/VR AntiCheat
The most reliable and secure publicly available Among Us 3D/VR anti-cheat


This anti-cheat was made because all other anti cheats are unreliable or just don't fully work and seem "unfinished". Every module in this anti cheat is tested before publishing, I also made this to help streamers and other players play Among Us 3D/VR without worrying about cheaters joining and ruining the game.
With this anti cheat, your game will be reliably protected from a large variety of known and advanced cheats (listed at bottom)

# How do I toggle the GUI?
You can toggle the GUI by clicking Left Ctrl on your keyboard.

# When can I use this mod?
You can use the Anti-Cheat while you are host, public or private rooms.

# Will I get banned for using this mod?
No, you will not be banned as this mod is simply to add protection to the game and help people prevent cheaters from ruining the fun.

# How do I use the Anti-Cheat?
First, download melonloader from https://melonloader.co/
Then open it and select Among Us 3D or VR and install
Then go to your Among Us 3D/VR folder and drag the AntiCheat.dll from the GitHub releases to the "Mods" folder, and start your game (It will take awhile to load in for the first load after installing melonloader)

# Is this malware?
No, it is fully open sourced and you are free to look at the code for yourself

# Can I whitelist my friends to prevent the anti-cheat from validating them?
Yes, the anti-cheat GUI has a Whitelist tab on the left side where you can add people to the whitelist who will be immune to the anti-cheat

# What happens when a cheater is detected and what if the cheater has a "Anti-Kick"?
The cheater will be kicked & blacklisted from your rooms upon being detected, even if the cheater has a "Anti-Kick" it will still forcibly remove them from your game and prevent them from joining in the future.

# I don't want a specific module to be enabled
You can choose which Anti-Cheat modules/verifications in the Anti-Cheat tab, toggle the GUI by clicking Left Ctrl.
